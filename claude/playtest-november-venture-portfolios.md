# November playtest — businesses and social account portfolios

This pass addresses notes 13–14. The current catalog caps ownership at three
businesses and four channels, so those are the largest playable portfolios
tested. No save migration.

- A business at its normal staffing level can cover its regular operations
  without its owner on the floor. Below normal staffing, capacity can still
  benefit from direct owner help. The owner’s stats and attention still affect
  demand, and minimum staffing, wages, weak markets and business losses still
  matter. The forecast and annual settlement share the capacity rule.
- Social accounts have no effort selector or required spend. The formats the
  player publishes determine activity; old saved effort settings no longer
  affect reach, yearly earnings, or workload in the live posting system. An
  account that did not post this year adds no workload. Active accounts still
  add to workload, and a manager still reduces it.
- Existing fame still expands discovery on successful posts. Poor, good,
  great and viral reception still depend on the post's own roll. Annual
  settlement does not publish on the player's behalf or roll another breakout.

The paired business test settles the same mature cafés alone and in a
three-business portfolio with a day job, holding skill, market, and each
company's events constant. The channel test settles four active accounts
together and separately and compares each audience and total gross earnings.
The advisor requests in note 15 remain separate.
