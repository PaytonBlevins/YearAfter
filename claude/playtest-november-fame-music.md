# November playtest — fame and music

Follow-up to the November usability PR #29. Save v58 stays; no migration.
This draft tackles the related fame, music reach, nomination, collaboration
and post discovery findings. A separate review of offer types and base pay
at the same fame score is still open, as are the business, auction and
advisor engine passes.

## Player changes

- Separate audiences combine with diminishing returns on the same fame gauge.
  For example, a music contribution of 31 plus an independent social
  contribution of 12 produces 39 rather than 31. Sports and its Laurels are
  one source. Music and modeling channels that already receive career
  crossover are not counted again as independent audiences. The creator
  phase's existing fame and decay are a floor, never another additive source.
- Fame helps discovery on posts that get a good, great or viral reception:
  30 fame adds 30% to positive gains before the platform cap. Poor reactions,
  follower losses and reception odds are unchanged.
- Once an artist has reached the national audience threshold and built a
  mature catalogue, distribution gives their work a second way to reach
  listeners beyond the original career-luck ceiling. The added runway uses
  current craft, a three-times national benchmark at craft 85, and a cap at
  the headliner entry point. It does not award listeners automatically.
  Idle artists still lose listeners; organic breakouts can exceed this runway.
- A newly released work that qualifies for a music award can lead to a
  losing nomination below 40 fame. Existing award quality and win odds are
  unchanged, and a quiet year or a year that already won does not add a loss.
- Music collaborations ease audience overlap with the square root of the
  existing overlap factor. Fees, repeat penalties and social collaborations
  remain on their own existing rules. This quotes lasting listeners, not
  streams. A music reputation row explains that industry standing and
  monthly audience are different.
- Existing fame work pays on the combined score. At exactly 32 fame the
  photoshoot formula still quotes $1,900; at 39 it quotes $4,250. A separate
  review of which kinds of work appear and how they pay for a given fame
  score is needed.

## Measured calibration

Three 150-seed paired production music-phase cohorts began with 400,000
listeners, a mature catalogue, reputation 85 and enough cash to fund the
plan. Fixed craft per cohort isolates the growth curve. Ten years of up
to two singles, an LP and touring per year. Earnings are phase proceeds
less recording and tour costs, before tax. This is a funded phase harness,
not a claim about a full-life household budget or a recreation of Payton's
save. Compared two-, three- and five-times national benchmarks; the middle
choice preserves an upside path without the strongest candidate's payouts.

| Craft | Median listeners after 10 years, before → after | Median annual net before tax, before → after | Reached 3M at least once, before → after |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------: | -------------------------------------------: | ---------------------------------------: |
| 65    |                                23,504 → 265,997 |                           $69,267 → $133,504 |                            0/150 → 0/150 |
| 75    |                                47,761 → 528,544 |                           $89,971 → $228,075 |                            1/150 → 1/150 |
| 85    |                               133,389 → 883,166 |                          $131,792 → $380,486 |                          15/150 → 16/150 |

The craft-75 ten-year listener p10/p90 moves from 31,380/1,005,918 to
507,602/1,142,865. Most of the improvement is sustaining earned reach; it
does not make every national artist an arena headliner. A checked-in holdout
cohort of 60 new seeds exercises the real growth path.

At 400k with a 700k-listener partner, a first collaboration converts
6,660 → 13,656 listeners; the third converts 1,665 → 3,414.
Music awards may be more common indirectly because more artists sustain
award-eligible audience tiers; in the craft-75 cohort the count of lives
with a win went from 17 to 67 out of 150. That is separate from the new
qualification route for losing nominations.
