# November — item and milestone art

A first pass at item detail illustrations on top of the visual refresh.
Artwork is decorative and does not change the item name, description, price,
auction quote, condition, or milestone text.

- Auction dialogs illustrate the lot. Storage units show only the exterior
  and size; the art never reads contents or hidden quality.
- Shops and collections share the auction artwork for the same catalog item.
  Watches vary by model, with digital, rectangular, angular, dive and classic
  styles, distinct palettes, and factory or aftermarket iced finishes.
- Vehicle listings, garages and auction lots use the same trim art, with a
  silhouette per body style and details based on trim and model.
- Graduation and award notices use dedicated scenes. Ordinary notices keep
  the shared illustration.

These are stylized vector illustrations composed from a common set of parts.
They are not faithful product drawings or individual paintings for every
catalog item. No save change.

The follow-up adds separate scenes for births and adoptions in the Life feed,
home purchase confirmations, military discharge, and the end-of-life summary.
Family scenes use the child's saved arrival age and route. This adds at most one
scene per age and does not add a popup or rewrite the timeline.

The next batch covers wedding, successful hire, and retirement confirmations.
Career recaps also get illustrations for music, acting, and modeling when
one of those fields has its own card. A recorded championship gets a medal
scene; awards still take priority when both appear in the same recap. Mixed
recaps without a featured milestone keep the neutral illustration.

The watch and jewelry pass assigns an art profile to all 55 watch catalog
models. Case shape, metal, dial, bracelet and visible face treatment vary
by model. The Jakob Timeless Treasure has a gold gem-set bracelet treatment.
The 42 rings, necklaces, chains, bracelets and earrings draw from separate
silhouettes and details such as stone sizes, bands, links, pearls and clasps.
The art is a stylized catalog depiction and does not add watch specifications
or change what an auction reveals.
